## Record a decision

After a maintainer accepts or rejects a proposal, add a decision record with:

- The date and a link to the proposal.
- The outcome and the maintainer discussion or pull request that records it.
- The reason for the outcome and relevant alternatives.
- The scope of the decision and any unresolved questions.
- The implementation owner and rollout plan for accepted changes.
- The compatibility policy, old-to-new mapping, and completion criteria.

Link the record from the proposal and update its status.
Distinguish **accepted** from **implemented** and **released**.
Keep the relevant release links with the record as implementation proceeds.
